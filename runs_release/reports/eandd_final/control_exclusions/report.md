# Question-only control exclusions

Generated by `scripts/analyze_control_exclusions.py`; do not edit by hand.

workspace: 11 of 72 runs (3 answer-bearing, 6 seed-project, 2 other-artifact; see `excluded_question_only_runs.csv` and `README.md`).
